Subject: Filing of MCP (Planned) and FCR Begins This Week

Team,

Starting this week, filing of MCP (Planned) and FCR begins in the MBT Sales Operations app.

Please prioritize the following:

1. **Profile your accounts** — starting with your active 1st tier accounts, then your new high-potential accounts. Only accounts you've registered under Account creation will show up as options when you file an FCR, so this needs to happen first.
2. **Plan your week or month ahead** — create your MCP (Planned) in the system and use it as your coverage guide going forward.

A few reminders on how the app works:

- Only **acknowledged** FCRs (where the customer confirms the discussion) can be approved and exported as PDF.
- Approved FCRs automatically populate your **MCP (Actual)** — one less thing to track manually.
- Every **Friday at 3PM**, the app emails all approved FCRs and the MCP (Actual) to the corresponding approver: the **NSM** for the MBT Sales Team, and the **Commercial AC Head** for the BD Team.
